# UX review: web app, landing, docs and preview banner (2026-10-02)

Large-scale review of every human-facing surface, run on 2026-10-02 against the live platform and
the mock backend, fixed in 16 commits that day (plus one dev-proxy commit), and finished on 2026-10-03 with a re-check of the
five most important surfaces (2 more fixes). Design rules: [DESIGN.md](../../packages/web/DESIGN.md)
and [design-impeccable.md](../research/design-impeccable.md).

**Result:** 26 findings fixed (1 P0, 9 P1, 14 P2, 2 P3), 6 open (all P3). No P0 or P1 is open on
the tour, change list, evidence, merge queue or landing hero.

## Scope

57 URLs × 2 widths (390 × 844 phone, 1440 × 900 desktop) × 2 themes (light, dark): 225 captures
(3 of the 228 combinations did not load).

| Group | Surfaces | Data |
|---|---|---|
| App, live | repositories, repository (change list, plans), members, plan, change/tour (Lane 1, Lane 2, reverted, revert, bootstrap, running, waiting for push), evidence, rollout (live, released), merge queue, audit log, agents, integrations, developer integrations, internal onboarding (time to first green), device login, OAuth consent error, 404s | Live platform, a signed-in dogfood account |
| App, mock | the same routes plus plan (approved), tour (running, failing), consent, install, claim, deliveries | `?mock` fixtures |
| Outside the app | landing, sign-in, bad claim link, docs (home, a guide, the error reference, 404), app 404 when signed out, preview gateway banner | Live platform |

## Method

- **Impeccable critique** per surface: Nielsen's 10 heuristics scored 0-4 (out of 40), cognitive
  load, the personas from design-impeccable.md §4 (the on-call reviewer on a phone, a power user,
  an accessibility user), and P0-P3 issues.
- **Impeccable audit**: `impeccable detect --json` over `packages/web/src` (1 warning, 13 DESIGN.md
  drift advisories, 0 errors) plus a manual pass for overflow, contrast in both themes, state
  copy and truthfulness of what each screen claims.
- **Live data first.** Mock fixtures are tidy; the worst problems only showed with real changes
  (failed tour generation, Lane 0 reverts, skipped measurements, audit rows from repositories the
  viewer can't see). Commit cd675f4 added `GF_BACKEND`, which points the dev server's proxy at the
  deployed platform, so the fixed UI could be checked against the same live data.
- **Severity.** P0: a person can act on a false or missing picture at a decision point. P1: wrong
  state, a false alarm, or a primary task hidden or broken on one width. P2: misleading or
  unhelpful copy, a degraded layout. P3: polish and consistency.
- **Before/after evidence**: `shots/before` (live deploy, 2026-10-02) and `shots/after` (fixed UI
  on the same data through the dev proxy, captured 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-03). 23 pairs plus one
  before-only shot, all light theme except the failing-change pair (dark). They are 256-colour PNGs,
  1.7 MB in total. The full 225-capture set was not kept.

## Scores

Impeccable critique score (Nielsen's 10 heuristics, 0-4 each, out of 40). The 2026-10-02 session
did not write its scores down, so the "before" column is reconstructed from the findings above and
the before shots. "Re-checked" surfaces were looked at again on 2026-10-03 (mock at 390 and 1440,
live data where noted, one dark-mode spot check); every other "after" is an **estimate** from the
fix list, not a new measurement.

| Surface | Before | After | Basis | What moved the score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tour / change page | 22 | 33 | Re-checked (mock 390, 1440, dark 390; live 390) | Status visibility and error prevention: no contradictory state, the diff is always there |
| Change list (repository) | 26 | 33 | Re-checked (mock 390) | Changes and plans first on phones; filters reachable |
| Evidence | 25 | 33 | Re-checked (mock 390, live 1440) | Judgments named; no false "raises the lane" |
| Merge queue | 30 | 34 | Re-checked (mock 390, live 1440) | Finished entries stop repeating themselves |
| Landing | 30 | 35 | Re-checked (390, 1440) | No overflow, copy buttons reachable |
| Repositories | 24 | 33 | Estimate | Honest empty copy, triage order, plans visible |
| Audit log | 22 | 32 | Estimate | No false integrity alarm; actions in words |
| Rollout | 28 | 33 | Estimate | Shared labels; no stale health line |
| Plan | 28 | 32 | Estimate | Plan text keeps its structure |
| 403 / 404 | 24 | 32 | Estimate | Human copy with a way to get access |
| Device login | 29 | 34 | Estimate | Command readable and copyable |
| Onboarding (internal) | 28 | 32 | Estimate | Chart readable on phones |
| Docs | 31 | 34 | Estimate | Reference tables readable |

Not scored: sign-in, claim, members, agents, integrations, consent and install had no P0-P2
finding; the preview banner is one line (its fix is finding 25).

`impeccable detect` (2026-10-02): the one warning (`side-tab`, `styles/queue.css:325`) is a false
positive, a CSS border triangle between batches rather than an accent stripe. The 13 advisories are
DESIGN.md drift: `#000` six times in `landing/styles/tag.css` and `loop.css` (likely mask colours),
radii of 1px and 4px (`landing/styles/hero.css`, `styles/onboarding.css`), and four font sizes off
the ramp (`consent.css`, `integrations.css`, `onboarding.css`). None was changed (open item 6).

## Open items

| # | Surface | Sev | Issue | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence, signature block | P3 | The `verify-attestation` command is a scroll box with no copy button, unlike the device page. | Render it with `Command` (`components/ui.tsx`). |

| 5 | Evidence, skipped plan judgments | P3 | The server's note reads "No plan is attached: not applicable (the classifier's plan rules decide)": two colons and internal phrasing. | Reword the note where the judgment is skipped (evidence producer). |
